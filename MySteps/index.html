1Overview

MySteps ("the app", "we", "our") is a step-count and fitness tracking app published by Dynamic Dragon. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it's used, and the choices you have. The short version: your step data is tracked and stored entirely on your own device — we don't run servers that collect or store your personal fitness data.

In one sentence: MySteps reads your phone's motion sensors to count steps locally, keeps that data on your device, and shows ads through Google AdMob to support the app being free.

4How we use your data

  • To count and display your daily steps, distance, calories, and active minutes.
  • To track progress toward the step goal you set, and your day-to-day streak.
  • To show the optional home-screen widget and goal-progress notifications you choose to enable.
  • To display a rewarded ad (via Google AdMob) when you choose to reveal an FAQ answer.

We do not use your step or motion data for advertising targeting, profiling, or any purpose beyond showing it back to you inside the app.

5Where your data is stored

All step history, goals, and profile settings are stored locally in the app's private storage on your device, using Android's standard app-storage mechanism. MySteps does not operate a backend server, and your step data is never uploaded to us or to any cloud service we control. Up to 90 days of daily history is kept on-device; older days are automatically trimmed to save space.

6Third-party services (ads)

MySteps uses Google AdMob to show a rewarded video ad when you choose to unlock an FAQ answer. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and basic technical data (such as device type and general ad-interaction events) to serve and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

No other analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs are used in this app.

7Data retention & deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed. Uninstalling MySteps permanently deletes all locally stored step history, goals, and settings — we hold no copy of it elsewhere to delete on our end. You can also clear today's count at any time from Settings > Test tools > Reset today.

8Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only data shared with a third party is the advertising identifier shared with Google AdMob, solely to serve the reward ad described above. We do not share your step, health, or profile data with any advertiser, analytics provider, or other third party.

9Children's privacy

MySteps is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

10Security

Since your data stays on your device, its security depends largely on your own device's protections (screen lock, OS updates, etc.). We follow standard Android practices for storing app data privately, so it isn't accessible to other apps on your device.

11Your choices & controls

  • Turn background step counting on or off anytime in Settings.
  • Turn goal-progress notifications on/off, and choose your own thresholds.
  • Revoke the motion, notification, or network permissions from your device's app settings at any time.
  • Reset your step count or uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally, for example if we add a new feature that changes what data the app accesses. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the latest version. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
